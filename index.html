    What it does, exactly

    Each page you drop is scaled to 23.81% and placed in one cell of a 4×4 grid, left to right and top to bottom. Sixteen cells fill an A4 sheet; leftover cells are left blank at the end of the last sheet. Pages are placed as pages, not as pictures, so nothing is re-rendered and no quality is lost.

    The grid is inset 5 mm from the paper edge so ordinary printers do not clip the outer codes. That makes each cell 50 × 71.75 mm rather than a true A8 of 52 × 74 mm. Print at 100% / actual size — "fit to page" will shrink it again.

    A hairline grey line marks every cut. A page that is not A4 portrait still gets placed — fitted and centred in its cell — and is named in a warning above.

    Small text on the source page shrinks to a quarter of its size and may stop being readable. Check the sheet before printing a hundred of them.
